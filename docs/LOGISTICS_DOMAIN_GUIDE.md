# SOA Guard AI — Logistics Domain Guide

## 1. Statement of Account (SOA) in Freight Forwarding
In international logistics, freight forwarders operate through global agent networks. At the end of billing cycles, destination or origin handling agents issue an SOA aggregating all job invoices, terminal handling charges (THC), freight, storage, and clearance fees.

## 2. Verification Challenges
1. **Tariff / Contract Variance**: Agents billing spot rates rather than negotiated contract rates.
2. **Unauthorized Charge Heads**: Invoicing for unapproved CFS storage, demurrage, or admin fees.
3. **Weight/Volume Discrepancies**: Incorrect chargeable weight calculations (e.g. 1 CBM = 1000 KG ocean vs 1 CBM = 167 KG air).
4. **Duplicate & Ghost Invoices**: Invoicing previously cleared jobs or jobs that were canceled.
